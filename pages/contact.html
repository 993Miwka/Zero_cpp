FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to create a mobile website?

The timeline depends on the complexity of your project. A simple mobile-optimized website can take 2-4 weeks, while a more complex solution with custom features may take 6-12 weeks.

Do you offer maintenance services?

Yes, we offer comprehensive maintenance packages to ensure your website stays up-to-date, secure, and performs optimally on all devices.

What is your pricing structure?

Our pricing is project-based and depends on your specific requirements. We offer free consultations to discuss your needs and provide a detailed quote.

Can you help with SEO?

Absolutely! We build all our websites with SEO best practices in mind and offer additional SEO services to help improve your search engine rankings.

Do you provide hosting services?

Yes, we offer reliable hosting solutions optimized for mobile websites. We can also work with your existing hosting provider.

What technologies do you use?

We use modern web technologies including HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), and frameworks like React, Vue, and Angular for building high-performance mobile websites.
